\makerubrichead{Research Profile}
I study how AI agents reason, learn through interaction, and continually improve while remaining consistent, safe, auditable, and aligned with human needs.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Reasoning and learning:} post-training, self-improving agents, continual and multi-agent learning, and strategic interaction.
\item \textbf{Consistency and introspection:} factual consistency, context attribution, and the influence of evidence on agent decisions.
\item \textbf{Trustworthy AI systems:} safety, security, prompt-injection defense, adversarial evaluation, and auditing of tool-using agents.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{CAST research agenda:} Consistent, Adaptive, Strategic, and Trustworthy agents.
