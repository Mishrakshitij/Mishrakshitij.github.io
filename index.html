01 / About

From conversation
to capable agents.

I am a Postdoctoral Associate at the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence, working on agents that improve through interaction while remaining consistent, auditable, and under human control.

I received my Ph.D. from IIT Patna, where I studied reinforcement learning for conversational AI, including dialogue planning, persuasion, politeness, empathy, and multilingual interaction. These questions continue to shape how I think about agents: what they learn from people, how they make decisions, and whether their behavior deserves our trust.

My experience spans research and deployed systems, including language-model editing at Amazon, reasoning in small language models at Quantiphi, the Etihad Conversational Booking Agent, and the Sevak Indian-language chatbot initiative.

02 / Research agenda

Four properties.
One connected question.

How can agents learn and pursue long-term goals while preserving consistency, safety, auditability, and human control?

Select a theme to explore

C / Consistency & introspection

From evidence
to reliable decisions.

How can an agent revise its beliefs when evidence changes, explain what influenced its decisions, and carry what it knows into consistent actions?

  • Factual and behavioral consistency
  • Context attribution and provenance
  • Memory, belief revision, and action alignment

Consistency gives adaptation a foundation: new experience should change the right beliefs without erasing valid commitments.

Current questions

Learning from textual feedback. Reasoning through investigation. Faithful attribution. Strategic interaction and adversarial auditing.

Looking ahead

Extending these questions to vision–language agents, evidence-bearing memory, cross-modal belief revision, and reliable long-horizon interaction.
