Kshitij Mishra

I am a Postdoctoral Associate at MBZUAI, studying how AI agents reason, learn, and interact. A central question guides my research: how can agents continually improve while remaining factually consistent, safe, auditable, and aligned with human needs?

My current work connects reasoning and post-training with self-improving agents, continual learning, and multi-agent learning. I investigate how agents learn from feedback and one another, how context shapes their conclusions, and whether what they learn transfers to new situations. I also develop methods for adversarial evaluation and auditing, with the goal of building safe and secure systems that use tools and act over long interactions. I welcome conversations and collaborations that connect AI with broader questions about intelligence, reasoning, adaptability, introspection, and trust.

My Ph.D. thesis at IIT Patna was titled Inducing Politeness and Empathy In User-Oriented Conversational Systems. In this work, I proposed new reward models to steer conversational agents toward desired goals. I also worked with two fellow researchers to deploy 14 multilingual conversational systems as part of Sevak, an Indian-language chatbot, covering railways, judiciary, and healthcare.

Outside research, I enjoy traveling and driving around India, spending time with family and friends, and reading about history and philosophy.

CAST research agenda

How can agents learn and pursue long-term goals while preserving consistency, safety, security, auditability, and human control?

Building trustworthy AI systems. I study how to make agents safe, secure, and auditable as they learn, use tools, and act over long interactions.

C / Consistency & introspection

From evidence
to reliable decisions.

How can an agent revise its beliefs when evidence changes, explain what influenced its decisions, and carry what it knows into consistent actions?

  • Factual and behavioral consistency
  • Context attribution and provenance
  • Memory, belief revision, and action alignment

Consistency gives adaptation a foundation: new experience should change the right beliefs without erasing valid commitments.

Current questions

Learning from textual feedback. Reasoning through investigation. Faithful attribution. Strategic interaction. Safe and secure tool use, prompt-injection defense, and adversarial auditing.

Looking ahead

Extending these questions to vision–language agents, evidence-bearing memory, cross-modal belief revision, and reliable long-horizon interaction.
